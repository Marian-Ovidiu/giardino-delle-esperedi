## 3. Product hierarchy

### Protagonist — the ingredient, not a SKU

**Mais Rosso Ottofile Integrale, varietà "Albese"**

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Nickname | *"il mais del Re"* |
| Why | Re Vittorio Emanuele II ne impose la semina |
| Morphology | Pannocchia with **exactly eight rows** of kernels |
| Kernels | Rounded, orange, rich in starch and protein |
| History | Grown in Piemonte until around the middle of the last century |
| Status | **At risk of extinction**; recently recovered, grown by "un'esigua minoranza di agricoltori" |
| Nutrition | Source of folic acid and vitamin B1; contains iron and other minerals; particularly digestible; rich in dietary fibre |

The number **eight** is a gift: a hard, verifiable, structural fact that can become a grid, a rhythm, a counting device, a signature interaction. No competitor is using it.

| 2 | **Maissini** | Grissini di mais, made with Mais Rosso Ottofile flour | — |

## 4. Current visual identity — what exists

- **Logo** — wordmark, black on light / white on dark. Simple, no strong lockup.
- **Palette** — default warm yellow/orange associated automatically with maize. Applied by convention, not decision.
- **Typography** — generic WordPress theme sans. No display face, no editorial voice, no scale contrast.
- **Imagery** — mixed-provenance product and agricultural photography, inconsistent lighting, colour and crop. No treatment system.
- **Layout** — stacked full-width modular WordPress blocks; centred text; equal-weight sections; galleries.
- **Motion** — essentially none.

**Reusable from the existing identity:** the name, the myth text, the eight-row fact, the "mais del Re" story, the Cherasco/Langhe geography, and the contact block. **Nothing visual is worth carrying forward.**

## 5. Weaknesses of the current website

1. **The myth is stated, then wasted.** The strongest brand asset in the business is a paragraph in the middle of the page.
2. **Flat hierarchy.** Mission, myth, corn, recipes, amaro and contacts all carry roughly equal visual weight. Nothing is the protagonist.
3. **The eight rows are never expressed.** The defining structural fact of the product exists only as a sentence.
4. **Automatic yellow.** Corn → yellow, with no art direction on top.
5. **Virtue-word inflation.** *Eccellenza, passione, autentico, genuino, unico* recur without evidence. The concrete facts (a king's decree, near-extinction, eight rows, stone milling) are far stronger and are buried under them.
6. **Recipes outrank products.** Two polenta recipes occupy more page than the three things the company sells.
7. **No product presence.** The range is a small gallery of format thumbnails.
8. **Outdated.** Last modified February 2024; the product range shown is no longer the one on shelf.
9. **Zero motion, zero rhythm, zero memorability.** Nothing about the page is recallable ten seconds after closing it.

## 6. Opportunities

**For the client**
- Make the *ingredient* the hero and the three products its consequences — this is a provenance brand, not a snack brand.
- Convert near-extinction from a footnote into the emotional core: buying this food is what keeps the variety alive.
- Correct the range to current packaging.
- Lead with verifiable specifics (eight rows, a royal decree, stone-milled, symbiotic agriculture) instead of adjectives.

**For the portfolio**
- **The number eight is a genuine structural concept** — a real constraint to art-direct against, which is rare and immediately legible to a design jury.
- **The Hesperides myth licenses a non-obvious palette** — evening light, dusk, gold-in-darkness — which lets us escape the corn-yellow cliché *with a defensible reason*, not as arbitrary contrarianism.
- Heritage-agriculture material treated with museum/editorial rigour is under-served territory; almost all competitors are rustic-warm.
- Small verified fact set = short, confident copy. A discipline, not a limitation.

## 8. Limitations and risks

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **AI food imagery reads as fake** — waxy surfaces, impossible highlights, mushy kernel detail | High | Art Director reviews every batch against explicit rejection criteria; prefer macro, texture and low-key light over full "dishes"; reject rather than force |
| **Invented packaging/label text** in generated images | High | Hard rule: never generate branded packs. Products are presented via ingredient-led composition, abstract form and typography |
| **Credit exhaustion** (199.5 total, no headroom to be careless) | Medium | Cost-check before batches; cheap models for texture/background, premium only for hero moments; batch and review |
| **Thin verified fact set** | Medium | Treated as an editorial constraint — short, confident copy. `[DA VERIFICARE]` for anything uncertain |
| **Product photography of real packs unavailable** | Medium | Typographic and ingredient-led product presentation instead of packshots |
| **Concept dilution under implementation pressure** | Medium | Art Director holds a hard approval gate at prototype (Phase 6) and final QA (Phase 8) |
| **Motion cost on low-end mobile** | Medium | Motion Designer specifies the mobile variant explicitly; video dropped on mobile if it does not pay for itself |
| Old site's superseded product range leaking into copy | Medium | Recorded explicitly in §3 as forbidden data |
